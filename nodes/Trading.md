# Trading

Trading terms and other information.

---

### Order types

There are three order types:
- Market Order
- Limit Order
- Stop Order

1. Market order - buy or sell **immediately at the best available price**. Use it when you want the trade to happen right away and dont mind small price changes
2. Limit order - buy or sell **only at a price you choose or better**. Use it when you have a specific price in mind and are willing to wait until the market reaches it.
3. Stop order (stop-loss order) - becomes a market order **once the price reaches a trigger level**. Use it when you want to automatically limit losses or enter a trade only after the price moves to a certain level.

Quick examples:
- stock trading at $100
- market order - buy now at around $100
- limit order - buy only if the price drops to $95
- stop order - sell if the price falls to $90 to help limit your losses


### What is "shorting"

Shorting (or short selling) is a way to make money when you think the price of an asset will go down.
